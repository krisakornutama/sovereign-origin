------------------------------------------------------------
3. ACTIVATE YOUR LICENSE
------------------------------------------------------------
The license is a signed text token. Your machine verifies it locally with the public key above;
the app never calls a server, so activation works with the network cable out.

  a. Put your license.txt next to the application, or open the app's .env file
     (sovereign-os/core-api/.env — copy .env.example if .env does not exist yet).
  b. Add these two lines, replacing the placeholders:

       SOVEREIGN_LICENSE=<the single long line inside license.txt>
       SOVEREIGN_LICENSE_PUBLIC_KEY="-----BEGIN PUBLIC KEY-----\n...\n-----END PUBLIC KEY-----"

     The public key is the content of attest-release.pub.pem with real line breaks written as \n.
  c. Restart the app. Open the /system page: the license card must show your tier and the days left.

If the card says the license is not valid, the usual causes are, in this order:
  - the token was pasted with a line break in the middle (it must stay one line)
  - the public key still has real newlines instead of \n
  - the license is bound to a different machine ID
Send me the exact message on the card and I fix it the same day.

An invalid or expired license never locks your data: the app drops back to the Community Edition
and keeps running.
