# Reply scripts — three situations

Every reply ends with one clear next step. No payment details in public threads.

## 1. "Interested, but can you do it cheaper?"

Thanks for asking. The price is already the direct-from-developer price, so there is no reseller margin
to cut. Two things I can do instead:

1. Start with Home ($59, one machine) and upgrade later — you only pay the difference.
2. Try the free Community Edition first. It is the same codebase, so you can judge the fit before spending.

And if it does not run on your machine, tell me within 7 days of receiving the files and I refund the full
amount.

Which machine count are you actually at, and what do you want to run on it? I would rather point you at
the cheaper plan than have you overbuy.

## 2. "Can I see it before I buy?"

Absolutely — two options:

- The full tour, with screens and the honest limits: https://sovereignoriginshop.dpdns.org/buy-intl.html
- A 15-minute screen share where I open a real installation: dashboards, sensors, security panel, backups.
  Tell me a time window and your timezone.

I do not hand over the full build or a license before payment — the build is the product. The free
Community Edition is the trial, and the 7-day refund covers the rest.

## 3. "I paid" (with a screenshot or transaction ID)

Thanks — I can see the transaction. To finish the delivery I need:

1. The business or personal name for the invoice
2. The name to put on the license
3. If you want the license bound to one machine: the machine ID from the /system page
   (not convenient? I issue an unbound license first and bind it later)

Within 24 hours you get: the Windows build (portable exe and zip), `license.txt`, the public key, and a
one-page install guide. Then verify the build yourself against the published fingerprint on the release
verification page — do not take my word for it.

If it does not run on your machine, tell me within 7 days of receiving the files for a full refund.

## Payment details

Never post them publicly. Send them only after the buyer has chosen a plan, and only in the same thread
or email where you confirm the plan and the amount. Remind them once: if anyone sends different payment
details claiming to be me, stop and ask.
