License terms, in plain words

  • Signed, offline activation. The license is an Ed25519-signed token. Your machine verifies it without calling any server of mine — nothing to phone home, nothing to shut down later.
  • Machine-bound. The token is tied to a machine ID you send me. One free machine transfer per year.
  • 7-day refund. If it does not run on your machine, tell me within 7 days of receiving the files and I refund the full amount. No questions about why.
  • Self-hosted, your data. I have no access to your installation and keep no copy of your data.
  • Source available. The project is open-core on GitHub: you can inspect what runs on your machine before buying, and verify every release yourself against a published key fingerprint.

Questions buyers actually ask

  • Windows only? The packaged build targets Windows 10 and 11. It is a portable exe and a zip, no installer required.
  • Do I need internet? No. Updates and optional integrations do, the core does not.
  • Is this a subscription? No. Home and Business include one year of updates; after that the app keeps working with the version you have. Pro is perpetual.
  • Do you handle VAT or sales tax for my country? It depends on the path. On a direct invoice from me, no: I am an individual developer, so the invoice does not collect your local tax. When the card checkout runs through Gumroad, Gumroad is the merchant of record and collects and remits the sales tax or VAT that applies in your country.
